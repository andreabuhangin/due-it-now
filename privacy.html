Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

What we collect

Due It Now stores your name, email address and the tasks you create (title, description, category, priority, due date and time).

Google Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar, the app asks for permission to create, update and delete calendar events. It only adds, updates and removes events for the tasks you create in Due It Now. It does not read your other calendar events. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How we use your data

Your data is used only to run the app. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
